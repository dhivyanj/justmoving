**holding on to the last string of hope**, with a heavy hole in the chest, if only you'd understand...

i dont feel like ill continue like this... this numb feeling, i want an end...

at the same time being for someone who is as broken as me, is another kind of feeling. they too need help and shouldn't be dismissed. you can't just say 'i dont care' for those who are actually struggling.

i do value those who are with me for the pain, i dont expect them to be with me always, but i hope they will be there when i am crumbling from intense pain and with no hope to continue...

**what is the point of infatuatedly loving someone if you don't have basic empathy for those who are suffering and trembling from pain.**
