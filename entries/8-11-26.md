# healing core

i just woke up to what my inner child is whispering all these times, hes glad that i am taking my time to heal, even after all of these self-sabotaging and self-deletion thoughts. he waited, he always assures that i dont lose my hope. he is crying, i am too with him. this was for the past 9 years.

i always feel that i am not a man of my words. i change my stances, i feel bad doing so but i dont have the memory of it doing so. trauma survivors have a beautiful gift - metacognition. i have replayed it more than 67 times in my head and understood where did it go wrong. **all roads lead to childhood trauma**.

theres a quirk about depressing content you are missing about - **it disturbs the comforted and comforts the disturbed**. it is a gift that if you are disturbed by the content i like, because you are comforted and i am not so.

it is pain that your body traps you inside tears amd not a single tear drops down from your eyes.

i dont know how well to describe it but i am trapped inside. depression tied me in a chair with tight rope, anxiety shouts at my ear threatening me of my dear life and guilt counts my scars, cuts them, wounds them again, and adds salt on it. i cry in pain, i scream in agony, i shout and scream inside my body and no one can hear it. the only thing that comes out is cry of disgust and fear. when they all get away, i try my best to untie myself from the shackles, ran outside crying for help, only for others to see my bleeding body and then to get frightened and disgusted and use their weapon at me, a new piercing, a new wound. the wounds and scars were not made of guilt, they were made when i got out crying for help.

i dont know any other safe place, i get inside where i was before bounded and depression hold me gently, places me on the chair and ties me gently and tightly and i get stuck in the same loop.

i always feel like i am a manipulator, i feel like my words are not in my control, i am trapped inside, i am screaming, i saw the force which trapped me show multiple faces to everyone and i coudnt do anything but to scream inside, *crying for help*. i know i am accountable for all which happened but i dont know what the actual bleep is happening in me, every simple gesture were *cries for help*.

Dear LORD, redeem me from this shackles, deliver me, Oh LORD, My GOD, i am running out of blood.

may every unshed tears be a prayer in your feet, my LORD.
