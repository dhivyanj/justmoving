# the monster

it is the law of nature that a select people are ruined and tested to the core that they are inevitably left with two choices - to forgive the world and show gratitude to the world which has brutally destroyed his entire sense of safety and his will to continue his life further, or to turn its back to the world. *if the world really didnt want me to survive the hardships it gave, if i survived, i will multiply its hardships and give it back what it deserved.* but i am on the path of a transitional state, i wanted a little bit of safety, i clung to people in bad and desperate ways, to finally realise that they belong to this same heartless world, which gave me nothing but pain pain and pain.

sometimes the world doesnt realise my breaking point, where do i stand, to be left with no safety nets, just moving forward every day just to survive. at the same time, it takes to be a mahatma to endure this pain but still show compassion to the world.

an entity as a monster or a villain - is a direct product of the same hardships and pain one has to go through. they say, kill the monster before you learn its story, else you will empathise with it and you will probably take its side. but think how the trauma of one should never be a reason to inflict trauma on others. as the stories say - once a monster is created by the same world - it will only inflict pain on others - the chain continues then.

**kill this monster immediately**, this entity didnt deserve this pain which it had to go through - but it never was a reason to desperately look for safety at the cost of inflicting pain on others. as you see the same stories - monsters who inflict pain are inevitably killed - so do the same to me - i was made the social outcast and to endure a pain only few had to go through, this should never be the reason that i choose to inflict pain.

at the same time look at the irony - the world which makes one endure pain at a stage which he couldnt have to go through - to face hardships to the core and looking at others who got all that they wanted - he wanted comfort and safety - he does not know what actual safety means - he resorts to things which only made matters worse - he clung to people for safety - nothing worked and made matters worse - he goes to the point where he starts bleeding - when the world has given him nothing but pain pain pain, even though he tries his best, his unsafeness leaks out and he is still marked as a social outcast - and when finally he snaps - he shows the world what he got to endure

*but what about heroes, they underwent the same struggles*, in a transitional state - where i broke down but i try my absolute best to not inflict pain and discomfort - it becomes a literal war inside where my only objective is to not fold in to the pain which has been caused but everytime i try, something goes wrong and i am labeled a villain - for what, to just leak out a tiny amount of pain which this world has caused to me.

i dont know about *this too shall pass* but i am eager for *i too shall pass*.
