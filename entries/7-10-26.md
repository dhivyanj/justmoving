# may death be kinder

how do i say that my hurt self can't keep people and i always self-sabotage whatever i got. i want people to stay but a part of me keeps them away. living in a paradox of pain, tires me.

what stage of healing it is when i cant feel any deadlines, simply numb of what i have to do, and it is killing me.

if you gave reasons to leave me or if i panicked and defended in front of you, i lost everything, i lost my will to live, i am gathering all remaining reasons to stay, now i have no reasons to stay. if there is no reasons to stay, may death be kinder.
