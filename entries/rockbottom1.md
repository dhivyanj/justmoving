# fruits of rock bottom

those who have ignored your cries for your help while you were drowning in the waters have no rights to know what the sharks did to me and i finally bled inside the waters.

the blood, it calls upon me, for what i have sown, now, it wants my blood, i know i can repent, but it keeps calling me, the accusations are valid, but i know it is not the solution

life will always humble you, so dont accept the fact that this will be always the same, **expect the unexpected**.

and at times, heart feels so heavy that both staying dormant and moving front feels sharp and starts to bleed.

what step of healing journey is this when i cant feel that i am going to back 2 courses, but inside i dont feel pain, instead i feel numb with tears stuck inside my eyes. **i am broken with no way to fix myself**. seems like all pathways are broken. even if you say all pathways can be made, i dont have the energy to fix them.

sometimes it does not mean that i am making others use me, it was just always **my cries for help**.

maybe i should be a bit distant on who stays and not. you will have people to need you only for their profits. your cries for help wont be considered at all.

idk how do i end this at it is a compilation of my grief. they said grief that lasts more that 6 months is pathological and must be medicated. forget april, it is kind of november now.

listening to bittersweet music gives another unique feel that it is all going to end soon.

if going through hell and out is healing, i want a quicker end - me or the pain.
