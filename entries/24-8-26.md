# the path to healing

the small hope of living, having a small candle flame inside me, which made me get through things. but what is it about, healing like an eagle, to pass everyday with no support whatsoever, to just get along with people and get through things only for the sake of getting the work done, and not as someone who would try to get along, to just be there.

the more you try to ignore it, the more stiff your muscles become, more your bones shiver, more your heart feels heavy, and more you lose track of things which excited you before. the more you get through it, your path becomes more narrower with both the sides being life or death, where a small slip makes you fall into the eternity. the small hope for living, where you gather reasons for how to continue life, till when I can ignore this unbearable pain, to the point where all my energy is spent just to hold my life together

the never-ending cry of my inner child who only saw pain throughout his life, where his only hope in life was, as soon as he doesnt give up, there was a small hope that things will become better soon. and oh boy what is it now, he is still crying that if i folded in by any chance, ill start to cry for no actual reason.

idk if its a joke or not but as i was already suffering, i tried to let go of what the past was holding me of. but at the same time, the past came through with a sharp striking hit at where i was wounded, a wound which was slowly healing, which was caused by the very same people who never stayed when things got harder for me. it was always me, holding myself when things got worse, bit by bit, inch by inch, until i lose all my energy trying to hold it all. *i cant take it anymore longer*.

to address the allegations: yes y'all want gossips, just smack a template and make it another masala story. the plot wasnt about moving on, but rather it was a hole, deepened by what i trusted the most, the more i trusted, the more i bled in front to share my burdens, my hole and my wounds deepened harder. to be wounded to the point that recovery is a hard alternative to something far worse

to this repository, which just acted as a mere wall, just helped me just write and type out my burdens. as if someone is going to read and whats the actual situation. at the same time, the more i bleed towards someone, the more i bleed by their attacks, and my mere inner child did nothing but cry cry cry...

the more i write these entries, the more invisible i become towards the society, more i get ignored. the society claims to be there to those who are wounded, but in reality it is the stark opposite. no one tries to come near a wounded person unless they have a weapon on their own

*until i write again...*

*(written and typed with unshed tears...)*
