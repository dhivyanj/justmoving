# मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय

saying it towards death, which is waiting for me, i know the journey is still far, there is a lot of distance to travel, to conquer what is left.

holding on to dear life, one step at a time, one pace at a time, with hopes that it will all end soon. 10 months for the love of my child.

i tried my best getting up myself, consoling myself that it is all for the glory which is to be attained. for the blood, flowing from my body, the war, the scars, the battle. **the hardest of choices needs strongest of the wills**.

what it is slowly rotting away my will to live or did i open my heart in ways i shouldnt have done them.

here i have a strong mould of a heart and i have molten steel of highest strength and the strongest and hardest of the metals. pouring into the mold and i start to hit it as soon as it hardens. where every hit on the heart denotes every single time i lost people, i pushed people away, i sat alone with no where else to go. every single step i made to make myself comeback slips me 3 steps below.

everytime i ran hopeless, every shortcoming, every misunderstanding, every time i try pushing forward, i fall back harder. 

with the same wry, i hit the heart with the power hammer with such a force that my heart doesnt take things that much anymore.

yeah the 12 points - dont overexplain, dont expect anything, etc..., **what is the point of having a heart so brittle as it was made from mere dirt**.
