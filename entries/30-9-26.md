# being chosen and looking for reasons to stay

first of all, those who stayed, **thanks for being for me as a reason to stay**, where i am in an unpredictable state of standing on a tightrope where a **single slip could make me give up**.

they say i feel this way *because it happened so*. my sibling in christ, i felt always this way, its that ive clung to people whom i have kept as **reasons to pass** on this day.

i always say, if theres no *hope*, **theres always cope**. hoping that we will take care of someone with no expectations, only their presence when things get hard. if theres no one, theres always *cope*. coping with what i loved to do, **with what makes me numb** for a while, cuz the last option is a *rope*...

when no one was there, if you stayed, **thanks for being as a reason to stay** and give me hope to pass on this day. if you *refused to do so* - as if you value yourself so much, if you say so, if something unexpected happens to me, ill try my best for making sure that *not a single drop of blood falls on your hands*.

i guess the *pretty privilege is real*, you always have people to validate you if you are so. when it all wears off, theres nothing to give.

theres *nothing to give back when you have nothing to give*. maybe ill wait for the time when life humbles you so much and then you realize **the real value of what does it mean to stay with someone**.

we make mistakes, we fall down, but it shouldnt be like apathy towards those who have fallen. if you have been disgusted by them or you have mocked them so, *life will target you in humbling you*.

take the case of a spirit lighters, if one falls in water, the flint cant spark even though it has the necessary spirit to burn. but when another lighter steps in, gives its warmth, helps to *dry the wetness* of hopelessness, guilt and shortcomings, the lighter **burns again** with a new flame and somewhat brighter.

delusively looking at the small beam of light coming from the other far end of the tunnel *(or is there light at the end of the tunnel?)*

until i write again
