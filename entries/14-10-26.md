# into the glory 

once you have stepped into the battlefield, you must not care about blood or death, come what may, fight till the end. 

im tired fighting. i am scarred and bleeding. being able to continue is a matter of pure willpower. 

ive fought enough. i am in the battlefield, bleeding, cant get up anymore to stand up. but i shouldn't be bleeding to death, i should be standing up, fighting till my last drop of blood. and to finally die with 3 arrows in the chest, dying **into the glory**.
