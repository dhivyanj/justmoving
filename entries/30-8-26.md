and the worst part is, i wanted to get out of it, i wanted to start new, meet new people, turns out i was still bleeding, i wanted to feel safe once again, **on the path itself, i made you feel unsafe**. imagine getting your life force sucked to the point that continuing life feels useless and you complain that you are an anti-social element, which can only cause harm.

well, if continuing life like this running on reserve energy is the solution, id rather prefer a short ending tbh. idk how well i can continue.
