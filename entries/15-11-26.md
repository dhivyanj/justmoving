# blood and memories

they say enjoy the memories and let go of the past. what if i say, theres no memories, they were just photographic glimpses of what i went through. i cant feel it, they were bloody. the guilt, the small cries for help, i have nothing to say as for myself. now i see crossmarks on their face, just the marks in my wrists. its blurry, it repeats, faces in my eyes get blurry or get cross marked. im scared. im bloody scared. feels like the blood will not wash away.

its okay, we can show our marks to those who are close, but when it is shown to the wrong person, it will be realised only when the damage is already done.

wrong people are like predators, like tigers or sharks. they **dont care** who and why are they bleeding, they look for the smell of blood, only to feast on the flesh.

its the blood ***𑌰𑌕𑍍𑌤𑌂***. sacrifice ***𑌬𑌲𑌿𑌦𑌾𑌨𑌂***. atonement ***𑌪𑍍𑌰𑌾𑌯𑌶𑍍𑌚𑌿𑌤𑍍𑌤𑌂***. it is calling, i dont want to go, but it is calling, the blood on my hands, i know, my hands are scarred looking for what i shoudnt have done.

the list of testimonies of my curse are growing, still in fact i coudnt hold on much longer of dear life.
