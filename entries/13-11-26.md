# not alone

shared joy is double joy, shared burden is half a burden.

the thing about grief is, it is not 'getting comfort in sadness' - grief is a quicksand, the more you fight it, the more it takes you in.

it is a wry that both can assure themselves they are *not alone* yet our mind and body refuses to accept we are safe.

going through something doesnt mean we disregard others pain.

every word here is an unshed tear.
